Before you place a single piece

Pre-flight checklist

  1. Place the Multi-launch coaster from the rides menu. Drop the station at the front-center of your 30×30 plot.
  2. Click the station → Edit track → toggle Advanced editor ON (button top-right of the editor panel).
  3. Zoom out and switch to a near-top-down camera angle. You can rotate camera with right-click drag — find an angle where you can see the whole plot.
  4. Turn auto-bank ON in editor settings — saves you from banking every curve by hand.
  5. Each new piece chains to the previous piece's exit. To change a piece type, click an existing piece → Change piece.
  6. The three colored arrows on a selected piece: white = stretch length, yellow = pitch (up/down angle), blue/red = yaw (turn angle). The banking slider rolls the track.

Reference cards

Settings, controls, and gotchas.

⚡ Launch Speeds

  • Launch 1: 45 mph — station exit, ground level
  • Launch 2: 50 mph — mid-cavern Tier 1
  • Launch 3: 35 mph — final boss approach

🚂 Train Settings

  • 3 cars per train (not 5 — compact loop is shorter)
  • Colors: car body black, trim red, seats dark gray
  • 1 train only — multiple trains can't safely cycle through such a short loop

🎛️ Editor Controls

  • White arrow: stretch piece length
  • Yellow arrow: pitch (up/down angle)
  • Blue/red arrow: yaw (turn angle)
  • Banking slider: rolls the track
  • Shift + place: mirror direction (left↔right)
  • Hold Ctrl: fine-grained adjustment

📊 Target Stats

  • Excitement: 7+ (good ride)
  • Intensity: 5–9 (guests will ride)
  • Nausea: under 6 (otherwise guests refuse)
  • Test-ride OFTEN — every 5 pieces, hit the green play button

⚠ Common gotchas

"Track must form a complete loop" error → pieces 25–27 don't quite meet the station entry. Zoom in, drag the white arrows on piece 27 by tiny amounts.
Train stalls on a climb → previous launch was too slow. Bump its target speed by 5 mph, or insert another Straight with boosters right before the climb.
Intensity above 10 → corkscrew or heartline roll is too fast. Add a brief Block brakes before it, or stretch the inversion's length so it happens slower.
Spiral doesn't fit your cavern → reduce the spiral radius in the piece options (try 4–5 studs). Track will be tighter but still legal.
Pieces 5, 7, 11, 16, 21 are your "tuning knobs" — these slight-slope pieces can be stretched or shrunk freely to land at the right height between major elements. If you're a few studs off after a spiral, fix it here.
